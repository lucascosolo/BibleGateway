## What it gives

A compact comparison of the canons: the Tanakh's three divisions with the Hebrew book names, the
Septuagint and the deuterocanonical books, and the differing Catholic, Orthodox (Greek, Russian,
Ethiopian) and Protestant lists, with tables. A convenient single citation for "which communities
count which books", to be checked against McDonald and Gallagher and Meade for anything
contentious.
