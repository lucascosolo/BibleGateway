## What it gives

The introduction defines a canon list, distinguishes lists from the contents of manuscripts and
from citation, and surveys the evidence the book then prints in full: the lists of Melito,
Origen, Eusebius, Athanasius, the Muratorian Fragment, the councils, Jerome and Augustine, with
the Jewish lists (b. Bava Batra 14b, Josephus). The full book, if the user obtains it, is the
primary text collection for the canon and collections view.
