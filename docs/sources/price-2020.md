## What it gives

Writing as a social practice in ancient Israel: what documents did, who used them, and what the
Deuteronomistic History assumes about written records, with the epigraphic evidence (including
what the record lacks, such as direct evidence for leather). Supports the "when could this have
been written" questions beside Rollston and Schniedewind.
