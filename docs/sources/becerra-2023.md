## What it gives

A readable survey of the New Testament canon's formation for a Latter-day Saint audience:
scripture versus canon, the Jewish scriptures of the first Christians, the role of Marcion, the
Muratorian Fragment, Eusebius's categories, Athanasius's 367 letter, and the criteria of
apostolicity, orthodoxy and widespread use. A secondary survey; prefer McDonald or Gallagher and
Meade for any claim that is contested.
