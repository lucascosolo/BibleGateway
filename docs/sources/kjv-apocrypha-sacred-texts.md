# KJV Apocrypha, Internet Sacred Text Archive (`kjv-apocrypha-sacred-texts`)

The 1611 King James Apocrypha as transcribed at https://www.sacred-texts.com/bib/apo/. Mapped to the
filed folder **`~/.cache/jot/sources/primary-canon-texts/`** (fetched 2026-10-08):
`sirach-001.htm` (sha256 `941e9faa5d19f9ed…`) and `sirach-001.txt` (`ee2832b48157d46c…`) for the
prologue of Ecclesiasticus; `sirach-prologue-kjv.htm`/`.txt` (`e01c3dbb1221130…`, identical files);
`2-esdras-14-kjv.htm` (`efa6dee8f12ab4be…`) and `2-esdras-14-kjv.txt` (`4f478a42e760967d…`) for
2 Esdras 14. Rights: KJV text public domain outside the UK (Crown letters patent there).
The whole KJV Apocrypha in the same text is also filed from eBible at
`~/.cache/jot/sources/outside/kjv-apocrypha/` (`eng-kjv_usfx.zip`, sha256 `8d11f96988d81746…`,
`text.txt` by chapter:verse) for any other verse.

Marker scheme: chapter:verse as the pages print them. No pages.
How to open a citation: 2 Esdras 14:45-46 is in `2-esdras-14-kjv.txt`; in the eBible copy,
`grep -P '^14:4[56]\t' ` within the `# book: 2 Esdras` section of `outside/kjv-apocrypha/text.txt`.
