## What it gives

The standard critical account of how the Hebrew Bible became a canon: the notion and use of
"scripture" versus "canon" (ch. 2, p. 39), the growth of the collections, the Septuagint and the
wider Jewish scriptures, Qumran, the "council of Jamnia" question, the rabbinic lists and the
Christian Old Testament with the deuterocanonical books. Dense documentation of ancient sources
and modern scholarship; use the index of ancient sources at the back to locate any passage.
