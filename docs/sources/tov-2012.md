## What it gives

- **Deuteronomy 32:8, pp. 248–249 (PDF 305–306).** Chapter 4 C, "Textual Transmission", the
  section on theological corrections. Table of readings: MT+ "the sons of Israel"; 4QDeut^j
  "the sons of God"; LXX manuscript 848 and 106c "sons of God" (υἱῶν θεοῦ); most LXX
  manuscripts "angels of God" (ἀγγέλων θεοῦ). Tov's judgement: "In its probably original
  wording, reconstructed from 4QDeut^j and [LXX], the Song of Moses referred to an assembly of
  the gods (cf. Psalm 82; 1 Kgs 22:19)", with 'Elyon and El possibly names of Canaanite gods
  (KTU 1.4 VI.46, the seventy sons of El and Athirat); "the scribe of an early text, now reflected
  in MT+, did not feel at ease with this polytheistic picture and replaced 'sons of El' with 'the
  sons of Israel'". A similar correction at the end of the Song (32:43) follows on p. 249.
- Index of ancient sources at PDF 497 onward lists every verse discussed; use it to locate other
  passages for later investigations.
