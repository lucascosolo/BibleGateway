Flavius Josephus, *The Complete Works*, translated by William Whiston (1737), in the Christian
Classics Ethereal Library edition (Grand Rapids: CCEL). Public domain. Supplied by the user on

## What it gives

The Life, the Antiquities, the Jewish War and Against Apion. Of direct use: *Against Apion*
1.37–43 (the twenty-two books "justly believed to be divine", the earliest statement of a closed
Jewish canon), *Antiquities* 11 (Alexander at Jerusalem, Ezra and Nehemiah), 18.63–64 (the
Testimonium) and 20.200 (James the brother of Jesus), and the War for 70 CE.
