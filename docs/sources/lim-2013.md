## What it gives

The Jewish canon from the Persian period to the rabbis: modern and ancient views of "canon", the
Letter of Aristeas and the Septuagint, Ben Sira, Qumran, Philo, Josephus's twenty-two books, 4
Ezra, and the rabbinic evidence, with Lim's thesis of a majority canon emerging by the end of the
first century CE.
