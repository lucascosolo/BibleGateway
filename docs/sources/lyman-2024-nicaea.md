## What it gives

A current scholarly survey: the diversity of pre-Nicene theology, the Alexandrian dispute
between Alexander and Arius (dated by Parvis to about 321/322), the council (Constantine as
facilitator rather than dictator; 200–250 bishops rather than the traditional 318; opening late May
or early June, ending with the Vicennalia feast on 25 July), the creed phrase by phrase with its
scriptural sources, the twenty canons, and the road to Constantinople 381. Full bibliography of
the Cambridge Companion to the Council of Nicaea (2021) and the standard monographs (Hanson,
Ayres, Parvis, Gwynn).
