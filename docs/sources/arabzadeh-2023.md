# Arabzadeh Sarbanani, "Revisiting the Book of Esther", Persica Antiqua 3/4 (2023), pp. 19–32

Open-access article (CC BY-NC-ND), supplied by the user on 2026-10-07. On disk at
`~/.cache/jot/sources/arabzadeh-2023/revisiting-the-book-of-esther-persica-antiqua-2023.pdf`
with `text.txt`. **Printed page = PDF page + 17.** The author is an MA in ancient Iranian
history at the University of Tehran; the journal is peer reviewed. The value of the piece is that
it tests Esther against Achaemenid inscriptions and administrative documents rather than
against Herodotus, and that it reports the state of the field plainly.
