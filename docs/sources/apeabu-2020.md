## Assessment (2026-10-10)

A teaching survey for a Nigerian college of education, with chapter questions. Its Nicaea pages
(printed pp. 176–183) are short and carry at least one plain error: "Constantine was baptized by
Arius on his death" (extraction line 5972). Constantine's deathbed baptism was by Eusebius of
Nicomedia; Arius had died in 335, two years earlier (Lyman 2024, section 3). **Not cited** in the
timeline content; Lyman 2024 and the primary texts cover the same ground. Kept filed in case a
later topic (the Donatists, the fifth-century councils) wants a general narrative to start from,
with every claim to be checked elsewhere first.
