Kitchen is an Egyptologist who defends the historical reliability of the Old Testament against
the "minimalists". The timeline records his positions under the **archaeological** tradition,
with his stance stated in plain language, because his arguments rest on Egyptian and Near
Eastern evidence rather than on tradition. Finkelstein and Silberman are the usual counterweight.
