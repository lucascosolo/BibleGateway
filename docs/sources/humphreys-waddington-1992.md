This paper restates and extends the authors' Nature 306 (1983) pp. 743–746 article and their
1989 chapter in *Chronos, Kairos, Christos* (pp. 172–181). It is the primary source behind the
3 April 33 CE position and is now cited directly; Chadwick 2015 and the Wikipedia articles
were the intermediaries before.
