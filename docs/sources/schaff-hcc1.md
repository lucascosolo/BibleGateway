## What it gives

Schaff's narrative of the apostolic age: Jesus, the apostles, Paul's journeys and letters, the
fall of Jerusalem, the composition of the New Testament books with his (conservative,
nineteenth-century) datings, and the apostolic fathers at the edge. Nothing on Nicaea beyond
passing mention; that is volume 3 of the series, not filed.
