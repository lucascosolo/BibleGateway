# Ehrman texts from the archive.org collection "apocryphal-gospels-bart-ehrman"

Six OCR text files (`_djvu.txt`) downloaded 2026-10-07 from
`https://archive.org/download/apocryphal-gospels-bart-ehrman/` at the user's direction, into
`~/.cache/jot/sources/ehrman-misc/`. They are single text files without form feeds; printed
page numbers survive as bare-number lines in the OCR, so a locator can be recovered
approximately by the nearest preceding page number, and should be marked "approx.".

| File | Work | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `brief-intro-nt.txt` | *A Brief Introduction to the New Testament*, 4th ed. (Oxford), c. 374 pp. | Dates and authorship for every NT book from the firmer critical side; counterweight to Brown 1997. The best of the six for the timeline. |
| `paul.txt` | *Paul: The Apostle. The Man and His Mission* (chapters 18–24 of *The New Testament: A Historical Introduction*) | Paul's life and letters; the undisputed/disputed split; chronology discussion |
| `from-jesus-to-constantine.txt` | *From Jesus to Constantine* (The Great Courses guidebook, 2004) | Lecture outlines; broad dates only |
| `christian-controversies.txt` | *The Greatest Controversies of Early Christian History* (Great Courses guidebook) | Lecture outlines; not a dating source |
| `heaven-and-hell.txt` | *Heaven and Hell: A History of the Afterlife* (Simon & Schuster, 2020) | Not a chronology source |
| `reliability-of-nt.txt` | Ehrman–Licona dialogue on the historical reliability of the NT (TheBestSchools.org, 2016) | A web dialogue; not cited |

The *Brief Introduction* is cited (`ehrman-brief-intro-2017`) by the New Testament composition
events, two issues, two persons and one work. **Its page locators cannot be re-checked**: no PDF
of it is filed under `~/.cache/jot/sources` (checked 2026-10-09), the OCR has no page breaks, and
the bare-number lines that stand for printed pages run in sequence but with gaps and stray
numbers, so no page offset is established. Every locator to it is therefore approximate, and no
entity may be graded claims-checked on its strength; the nine composition events that had been
(Romans, 1 and 2 Corinthians, Philippians, Philemon, Colossians, 2 Thessalonians, the Pastorals,
1 Peter) were returned to sources-located on 2026-10-09. A filed PDF, extracted per page, would
let them be checked.
