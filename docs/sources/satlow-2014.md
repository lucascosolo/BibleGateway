## What it gives

A historian's account, from the Iron Age to the rabbis and the early church, of when texts
acquired authority as opposed to when they were written: scribes in the monarchy, the
Deuteronomistic historian, Ezra, the Hasmoneans, Qumran, and the slow rise of "scripture". The
argument that holiness came late is the counterweight to the view that the books were
authoritative from composition.
