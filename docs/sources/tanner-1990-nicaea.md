# Tanner, Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils, Nicaea I (1990), via the St Michael's Depot compilation

Norman P. Tanner (ed.), *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils*, vol. 1 (1990): the First Council of
Nicaea's profession of faith, twenty canons and letter to the Egyptians, as reproduced in the
compilation "0325-1965, Concilia Oecumenica, Documenta Omnia, EN" (St Michael's Depot), a
1,635-page PDF supplied by the user on 2026-10-10 (`docs/sources/0325-1965,_Concilia_Oecumenica,_Documenta_Omnia,_EN.pdf`),
text extracted to `~/.cache/jot/sources-pdf/`. Tanner's translation is in copyright; the
compilation's own status is unverified. The PDF stays out of the repository and quotations are
kept short. The same texts are public domain in Percival's 1900 translation (`npnf2-14-ccel`),
which is cited beside Tanner wherever a quotation matters.

Nicaea I occupies PDF pages 1–8 (extraction lines 1–312); Constantinople I begins on the next
page. Cite by document and section ("profession of faith, section 2"; "canon 17"; "letter to the
Egyptians, section 3").

## What it gives

The council's three surviving documents in a modern translation, with Tanner's introductory note
(opening 19 June; presidency uncertain; 318 as Hilary's traditional figure against Eusebius's
250, Eustathius's 270 and Athanasius's about 300) and his marking of the council's additions to
the underlying creed.
