Carson and Moo are the standard evangelical introduction: they defend the traditional
attributions (Pauline Pastorals, Petrine 1 and 2 Peter, apostolic Matthew and John) with
arguments engaged against the critical consensus. The timeline records them as the
**conservative** voice on the composition axis beside Brown (moderate) and Ehrman (critical),
under `tradition = "critical"` because they argue from the same evidence, with their stance
named in the summary. Every chapter has sections headed AUTHOR, PROVENANCE, DATE, ADDRESSEES,
etc.
