# Percival, The Seven Ecumenical Councils (NPNF2 vol. 14, 1900)

Henry R. Percival (ed.), *The Seven Ecumenical Councils of the Undivided Church*, Nicene and
Post-Nicene Fathers, Series II, vol. 14 (New York, 1900). Public domain by date. Fetched on
2026-10-10 as CCEL's ThML source, `~/.cache/jot/sources/npnf2-14-ccel/npnf214.xml`
(https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf214.xml). Socrates's Church History (NPNF2 vol. 2) was
fetched the same way to `~/.cache/jot/sources/npnf2-02-ccel/npnf202.xml` and is not yet used.

Cite by Percival's section titles ("The Nicene Creed", "Excursus on the Word Homousios",
"The Canons of the 318 Holy Fathers", "Canon VI", "The Synodal Letter").

## What it gives

The Nicene creed, canons and synodal letter in a public-domain English translation, Percival's
historical introduction ("No Acts of either of the first two Ecumenical Councils have been
handed down"), the creed of Eusebius of Caesarea beside the council's, and long excursuses with
the ancient and modern commentary on each canon.
