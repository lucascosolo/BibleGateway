Dated scholarship (1912). Cite it for what it reports of ancient texts and for the history of a position; it does not stand in for current critical scholarship on dating.

Its regnal dates are those of 1912: Shalmaneser III's western campaign (Qarqar) is dated
854 BCE throughout, against the 853 now standard, and Tiglath-pileser III is "IV". Use those
dates only as the older reckoning they are.

## Where to look (from the table of contents, leaves 12-13; page numbers are the printed ones)

- Tell el-Amarna letters, including Abdi-Khiba of Jerusalem: pp. 258-282 (Abdi-Khiba 268-277).
- Shalmaneser III, the Qarqar campaign (dated 854 BCE here): Obelisk, Monolith, Bull and Berlin
  inscriptions, pp. 288-298; Jehu on the Obelisk (842 BCE), pp. 303-304.
- Tiglath-pileser III (Pekah, Samaria): pp. 312-322; the western campaign of 733-732, p. 317.
- Sargon II, the conquest of Samaria (722-721), pp. 323-331.
- Sennacherib's campaign against Jerusalem (701), pp. 332-348, with Herodotus, Polyhistor and
  Abydenus on it, pp. 346-347.
- Esarhaddon and Ashurbanipal, pp. 349-359; Nebuchadrezzar, pp. 360-370.
- Nabonidus, Belshazzar and Cyrus, pp. 371-384: the four clay cylinders of Nabonidus (Ur),
  p. 378; the Cyrus Cylinder, p. 380.
