## What it gives

A careful evangelical definition of inerrancy: distinguished from inspiration (origin versus
nature), "without error" versus "unable to err", and the qualifications about genre, authorial
intent and the standards of truth the authors themselves used. Useful as the attributed

so that the position is described in its own best words rather than by its critics.
