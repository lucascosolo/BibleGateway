# Eusebius, Life of Constantine (NPNF2 vol. 1, 1890)

Eusebius of Caesarea, *The Life of Constantine*, translated by Ernest Cushing Richardson, in
Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, Series II, vol. 1 (New York, 1890). Public domain. The volume was
already filed at `~/.cache/jot/sources/npnf2-01-ccel/npnf201.xml` for the Church History (ledger
`npnf2-01-ccel`); the Life is in the same ThML file, book 3 chapters 6–20 at lines about
64230–64700, read on 2026-10-10. Cite as "Life of Constantine 3.6" by book and chapter.

## What it gives

Eusebius's narrative of the council: the summons and the public transport (3.6), the bishops
"from all the churches which abounded in Europe, Lybia, and Asia" and the Pentecost comparison
(3.7–3.8), "the number of bishops exceeded two hundred and fifty" (3.8), the emperor's entrance,
the low gold chair and the speech for peace (3.10–3.12), the agreement on the faith and on Easter
(3.14), the Vicennalia feast (3.15), and Constantine's letter to the churches on Easter (3.17–3.20).
