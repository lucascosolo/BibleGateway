# Streeter, The Four Gospels: A Study of Origins (Macmillan, 1924)

Public domain. Downloaded 2026-10-07 from biblicalstudies.org.uk at the user's direction, 638
PDF pages with a usable text layer, at `~/.cache/jot/sources/streeter-1924/the-four-gospels-streeter.pdf`
with `text.txt`. **Printed page = PDF page − 21 up to about p. 483; from chapter XVII onward the scan's
pagination shifts and printed page = PDF page − 17.** Always confirm against the running head.

The classic statement of the Four Document Hypothesis (Mark, Q, M, L) and Proto-Luke, with
Streeter's dates and places for each Gospel. A century old, so it is recorded as a landmark
critical position, with the date of the book stated, not as the current consensus (Brown,
Ehrman and Carson–Moo give that).
