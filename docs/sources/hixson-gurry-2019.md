## What it gives

Fifteen chapters by evangelical text critics correcting popular claims on both sides:

- 1 Introduction (Gurry and Hixson), p. 1.
- 2 Myths about autographs (Mitchell), p. 26. 3 Math myths: how many manuscripts (Peterson), p. 48.
- 4 Myths about classical literature (Prothro), p. 70.
- 5–6 Dating myths (Hixson; Lanier), pp. 90, 110.
- 7 Myths about copyists (Cole), p. 132. 8 Myths about copying (Malik), p. 152.
- 9 Myths about transmission: Philemon from beginning to end (Solomon), p. 171.
- 10 Myths about variants: why most are insignificant and some cannot be ignored (Gurry), p. 191.
- 11 Myths about orthodox corruption (Marcello), p. 211, the direct reply to Ehrman.
- 12 Myths about patristics (Blaski), p. 228. 13 Myths about canon: what the codex can and can't
  tell us (Meade), p. 253. 14 Myths about early translations (Coogan), p. 278.
- 15 Myths about modern translations: variants, verdicts and versions (Ebojo), p. 304.
