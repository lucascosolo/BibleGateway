A Latter-day Saint scholar's case for Thursday 6 April 30 CE. Part of the year argument uses
the Book of Mormon (a 33-year lifespan, 3 Nephi 8), so the timeline records the position under
the **traditional** lens with that stated plainly. The article is still useful on its own terms
for its survey of the scholarship and its engagement with Humphreys and Waddington.
