## What it gives

The modern defence of the four-source model: the case studies (the flood, the complaint in the
wilderness, Joseph), the literary criteria for separating J, E, P and D, the compiler, and the
reply to the European supplementary and fragmentary models. The direct counterpart to Kitchen
and the technical complement to Friedman.
